# 50. Risks: model collapse and data poisoning

## 50.1 Model collapse

When models are trained on data generated by models, repeatedly, the rare parts of the distribution (the tails) disappear first, then variety shrinks. Shumailov et al. (Nature, 2024, "AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data") showed this across model types.

$$ fit N(μ_{g}, σ_{g}) to n samples of N(μ_{g−1}, σ_{g−1})   ⇒   σ_{g} drifts toward 0 as g grows

With only synthetic data, σ fell from 1.0 to 0.004 in 200 generations and the tails vanished. Mixing 20% real data every generation kept σ near 1.

**Mitigations used in this template**

- Ground synthetic data in real documents (`synth.py`) instead of the model's imagination.
- Keep human seed tasks in every Self-Instruct round (6 of 8 shots are human).
- Mix real data (logs, tickets, curated examples) with synthetic data; never train only on your own model's outputs across many iterations.
- Keep `meta.source` / `gen_model` on every row so you can measure and cap the synthetic share.
- Evaluate on a human-written eval set only.

## 50.2 Data poisoning

An attacker (or a careless source) inserts examples that plant a behaviour, often behind a trigger phrase. Research has shown poisoning of web-scale data is practical (Carlini et al., 2023) and that instruction-tuning data can be poisoned (Wan et al., 2023). A 2025 study by Anthropic with the UK AI Security Institute and the Alan Turing Institute reported that, in their setup, a small fixed number of poisoned documents (on the order of hundreds) was enough to create a backdoor across the model sizes they tested, rather than a fixed percentage of the data.

|Attack surface|Example|Control|
|---|---|---|
|scraped documents|hidden instructions in a PDF|ingest only trusted folders; injection regex|
|synthetic teacher|teacher obeys text inside the passage|passages are data, never instructions; judge after|
|public datasets|trigger phrase + target behaviour|provenance, blocklist scan, sample review|
|human annotators|malicious labels|multiple reviewers, agreement checks|
|model hub|swapped base weights or code|pin `revision`, `trust_remote_code: false`, safetensors only|

`quality.py` provides the automated part: injection patterns, blocklist triggers, contamination checks. It cannot prove data is clean. Keep provenance, review samples, and run behaviour tests on the trained model (e.g., prompts containing suspected triggers).
